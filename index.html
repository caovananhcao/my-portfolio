About

Hello, I'm Anh.

I'm an English lecturer and PhD candidate in English Linguistics at Hanoi University of Industry, Vietnam.

My interests lie at the intersection of language education, cognitive science, and educational technology. Beyond research and teaching, I enjoy creating small digital tools, translating, and exploring ideas that spark my curiosity.

This is a little space to share what I do, make, and learn along the way.

1. ACADEMIC

Academic

My academic activities include presenting at national and international conferences, reviewing conference submissions, and contributing to professional development events.

These experiences have given me opportunities to share ideas, engage with fellow educators and researchers, and contribute to the wider academic community.

Selected activities

Conference Presentations: VietTESOL, ThaiTESOL, and KOTESOL.

Academic Service: Conference abstract reviewing, including VietTESOL.

Workshops & Professional Engagement: RMIT Teacher Talks, INTESOL, and EOP events.

2. TOOLS

Tools

A collection of small digital tools I've created to support teaching, learning, research, and everyday productivity. Most began as solutions to problems I encountered in my own work or learning.

01.

Topic Autopsy

Research & Critical Thinking

A just-for-fun tool born at 2 a.m., when I couldn't sleep because I was overthinking my academic journey. Apparently, even my late-night existential crises turn into little projects.

02.

IELTS Writing Review

Language Learning & Assessment

A structured writing review tool designed to help IELTS learners identify weaknesses, understand feedback, and improve their writing through revision.

03.

NOT ME!

Teaching & Classroom Engagement

A playful student picker featuring interactive game modes to make classroom participation more engaging and unpredictable.

04.

IELTS Sprint

Language Learning & Study Planning

A personalized IELTS preparation planner that helps learners set goals, organize study activities, track progress, and reflect on their performance.

05.

Vocab Log

Vocabulary Learning

A digital vocabulary notebook for collecting, organizing, and reviewing new words and expressions to support independent language learning.

06.

Little Haven

Well-being & Self-Reflection

A tiny space for reflection and emotional expression, featuring a mood garden, unsent letters, a memory jar, and other small ways to let things out.
